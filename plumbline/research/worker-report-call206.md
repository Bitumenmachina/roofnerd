## Point 3 before any code (what the reader does)
- Reader today (crates/ifc/src/lib.rs ~885-975): a file level is matched to a job level only by NAME. Same name and floor within 0.01 ft: shared. Same name, other height: the whole model is refused. Any other name: a new level, even at a height the job has.
- A room's figures (building.rs `room_measures`): from EVERY wall of the job that spans the level's computation height (the level's elevation + COMPUTATION_HEIGHT_FT), whatever level the wall is on. So putting the file's wall on the job's level instead of a second level at the same height does not change a room's figures: the wall cuts the room either way. The consequence nobody was told is the wall, not the level; so the reply must name the rooms whose figures moved, worked by the model.
- "The same height": the floor height the file's level states (its elevation, not where it is placed), within 1/8 in (0.125/12 ft = 0.0104167 ft) of a level the job had before this import. Levels made earlier in the same import do not count (two levels at one height in one file stay two, as R21-30 has it).
- The living room by hand, from the job's walls: south wall (0,0)-(40,0) 6 in, hall partition (0,16)-(40,16) 4 1/2 in, west wall x=0 6 in, east wall x=40 6 in. Inside faces: x 0.25 to 39.75 = 39.5 ft; y 0.25 to 15.8125 = 15.5625 ft. Area = 39.5 x 15.5625 = 614.71875 SF. The judge's 614.7188 is right. The file's wall (24,0)-(24,16), 6 in, cuts it at x 23.75 and 24.25; the room's point (20, 8.03) is in the west part: 23.5 x 15.5625 = 365.71875 SF. The judge's 365.7188 is right (perimeter 2 x (23.5 + 15.5625) = 78.125 LF, as the program says). Receptacles are the program's rule (1 for every 12 ft or part of each run of wall 2 ft or more, cut at doors); 10 and 8 are what it works; I did not redo the doors by hand.
