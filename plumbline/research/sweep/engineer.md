# Structural engineer, opening a bearing wall (sweep, call 174)

Seen = pictures and the frozen command line (bin22). Not the live window. Beams, posts, pads, joists, rafters and the `structure` verb are NOT in bin22 (`$B structure` prints usage; usage list has no beam/post/pad); I judge them from DECISIONS calls 164, 165, 171 as written.
Pictures read (28): trades/1-foundation, 2-framing, 3-electrical, 4-plumbing, 5-hvac, 7-waterproofing, first-floor-alone; renow/03, 05, 08, 10; roofw/05; clash/03, 05; layers/03, 05; glass/06; field/03; house/05; scanw/06, 09; d2/05; foundw/05, 08; (all "-model.png"). Also run: sixteen -> show/check/vif/clashes/takeoff, mark demolish --keep + set bearing, export ifc.

## 1. Who I am and why I open this
1. What carries what: which walls are bearing, which way joists run and how far they span, what sits on what, down to the footing.
2. What happens if this wall comes out: what is above it, what the load goes to (beam, posts, pads), what is under those.
3. What is existing and unverified (sizes, species, grade, spacing, condition) versus measured; and what the builder assumed that I have not sized.
4. Before it is opened: a list of questions that must be answered (shoring, temporary support, hidden loads), not a price.

## 3. Missing or wrong for me (one line each)
- No "Structure" layer or switch: framing 16 is walls only. I looked for a floor system; got studs. Money: the cheapest-looking wall removal is the one that hides a girder.
- Bearing is not drawn: bearing and partition look alike in 3D and plan. A cheap colour/hatch for "said bearing / said not / not said" would let me see the whole building's assumption in one glance.
- No "what is above this wall" / "what bears on it". Command line: demolished wall-0016 (first floor, 12 LF at x=24) and second-floor wall-0051 at the same line stays up untouched; nothing says it is now unsupported; nothing links a wall to what is over or under it. I tried `vif`, `check`, `clashes`: silent. This is the whole point of my visit.
- "bearing" is a free word on any wall (`set ... bearing`) and accepted even when the wall is not opened (kept and shown). Fine, but it is Assumed by default for nothing: the program asks only of a wall the work opens. A wall not marked but with a new opening cut in it is asked (call 160). Good. A wall that is NOT touched but whose lower support is removed is not asked.
- Call 160 caution is good: "needs a beam, its posts and their footings, sized by an engineer, and shoring while it is out. None of that is in this price unless a line says so." I saw it in `check`/`show`/`estimate`. Right and honest. But it only appears once bearing is typed; the default of Unknown on an opened wall appears as a field-list line ("bearing: unknown", not seen priced), $0 riding on a wall with no lines priced: nothing prices the risk even as a range or a placeholder line.
- Joists: which way they run is not in the model in bin22. Call 165: default "the short way", Assumed, with the reason printed: good; but the DEFAULT drives bearing guesses (joists usually span the short way is exactly what fails in additions/old houses). Needs to be a field question, not a default; see below.
- Existing vs new framing: marks exist (existing/demolish/new/temporary), but no "existing, size unverified" for joists/rafters (lumber species, grade, 2x8 vs 2x10, sag, notching by plumbers). Existing is Assumed; fine; but no way to say "2x8 at 16, not verified, 12 ft clear span" anywhere in bin22.
- Tie of an addition to the old house: no concept of a joint between new and existing (ledger, footing step, differential settlement, roof tie-in, valley). The roof builder moves an eave and re-plans; nothing flags the tie.
- Beam (call 164): good principle (typed sizes, Known/Assumed, "an engineer sizes it"), weight per foot typed, count of "members whose size is not Known". Missing: a beam is only a run with a top elevation; no bearing length at each end, no "carried by" link to posts, no check that a post is under each beam end, none that a pad is under each post (not tested against each other, said so). A beam that ends in air is accepted.
- No point loads/line loads listed as questions even as words: roof snow region, floor use (bedroom/bath tile/stone, hot tub, aquarium, piano), tile/stone/green roof dead loads. A veteran would want an "assumed loads" card with Unknown rows (the program must not size, but it can list what must be told to the engineer).
- No notching/drilling conflict between MEP and joists: the clash test excludes framing bodies from walls and floors; with joists as counts only (call 165), a 4 in soil stack through a floor is tested against the floor slab but never a joist. Real money: sistered joists, headers around the stack.
- Footing: strip footing only follows walls above grade; no footing under interior bearing lines/posts in the sample; pad footing is in 164 but is not in bin22. Soil bearing capacity, frost depth, water table: no Unknown rows seen.
- Clash list: "in the same space" findings between a wall and fixtures at 0.3 to 1.8 in (water closet 1.80 in into the wet wall) are drawn-fit noise at the scale of a 6 in wall; they sort next to real conflicts. Wall vs fixture fits dominate; the structural things (wall vs duct crossing) are "through a wall: hole to cut" with "nothing priced rides on the hole itself", while a hole cut through a bearing wall's top plate or a joist is exactly the case that costs. Not distinguished.
- IFC export: 0 LoadBearing properties (STATUS.md 8472 admits), so a designer/engineer receiving the model sees no bearing flag; 20 IFCWALL(, no IFCBEAM etc. in bin22. Pset_WallCommon.LoadBearing exists in the standard; this is the one field I would read in an exchange.
- Top bar after demolishing a bearing wall: "1 to verify" and price shows "no figure"; the caution is in the panel. I want a red banner count: "1 bearing wall coming down: engineer needed".
- "Teach by doing" text in a free panel for a "foundation": nothing on the stacking of a lower floor.

## 4. What the best tools show today
- Revit: every wall has a Structural Usage (non-bearing, bearing, shear, combined); the Wall tool defaults to non-bearing and the Structural Wall tool to bearing; analytical model enabled per element. Source: https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2023/ENU/Revit-ArchDesign/files/GUID-09424EC8-02FC-4210-AA6F-9CD6598CFF48.htm ; Tekla import note that walls with the analytical model off are missing: https://support.tekla.com/article/when-i-import-a-revit-model-into-tekla-structural-designer-the-walls-are-either-missing-or-defined . None of these say a bearing wall's usage is Unknown; they default silently. This program already beats them on honesty (Unknown/Assumed with a source, a caution, a field list).
- Free sizing tools (Canfor Power Sizer, CWC Beam Calculator, Simpson Wall-Bracing selector) size members; the program rightly does not (AWC tables not reproducible, see research/renovation-structure.md). Source: found via search (calcs.com/beamcalc.cwc.ca/canfor.com). What none do: carry an unverified existing-framing record through to a price and a dated question list. That is where this could be ahead: an "engineer's packet" that exports exactly what is known, assumed, unknown, with photos/measure fields, for the engineer to size from, and takes the engineer's answer back as Known with the engineer named as source.
- Not checked on the maker's page (do not rely): any claim about what Revit does for load-path tracing.

## 5. My ten, in order
1. **Load path under a wall**: a click on any wall shows "carries: [walls/floor/roof above, by level] and rests on: [wall/footing/post/nothing below]" computed from the model's own plan overlap (this wall's line against walls/footings/floors on the levels above and below). Words: "Rests on: nothing found". Done when demolishing first-floor wall at x=24 lists second-floor partition at 24 as "now carried by nothing".
2. **Colour bearing**: a "Structure" switch that tints walls: bearing (said), not bearing (said), not said (hatched), each with its status mark; a count in the key. Done when the first picture of the whole house shows which walls nobody has said anything of.
3. **Joist direction as a question, not a default**: floor/ceiling "joists run" drawn as arrows on the floor and a field question "which way, what size, what spacing, what span" Unknown until said (not "the short way, Assumed"). Done when the VIF list carries the floor above any demolished wall with dollars riding.
4. **Engineer's packet export**: one sheet (PDF/xlsx): each wall/opening coming down, bearing status and source, what is above/below, existing framing sizes (Assumed/Unknown) with photos/measure boxes, loads asked (snow, use), shoring flagged. Reading back an engineer's answer: size typed, Known, source "engineer, date, letter number". Done when the sheet round-trips.
5. **Opened-wall banner**: top bar red chip "1 bearing wall or Unknown coming down: engineer, not in price" (not "1 to verify"); clicking flies to it. Done when renow/05 would show it without opening the panel.
6. **Existing framing record**: for floors/roofs marked existing: joist size, spacing, species/grade, span, notching/damage, each Known/Assumed/Unknown, same measure box as field/03. Done when a joist can be Known and a sister/replace line appears.
7. **Header sizing flag on every new opening in a bearing wall**: count of headers with "span, load: engineer" next to each (today header_depth is a rule of thumb, say so on screen). Done when a 6 ft window in an Assumed-bearing wall lists "header: not sized".
8. **Bearing and tie of an addition**: a "joint" between new and existing: ledger/tie, footing step, roof tie-in questions (settlement, flashing, ridge tie). Done when marking a wall New next to Existing raises these three Unknown rows.
9. **Structural clash classes**: MEP through a joist/header/top plate/beam is its own class with the money (sister, header, notch rules) separate from fixture-fit noise (sub-2 in, wall-vs-fixture). Done when the clash filter has "structure" and demotes sub-2 in fixture fits.
10. **IFC LoadBearing and Pset round-trip**: write Pset_WallCommon.LoadBearing = true/false/unset, IfcBeam/IfcColumn/IfcFooting with the status; reading an engineer's IFC brings the bearing flag in as Assumed (source named). Done when the IfcOpenShell check shows LoadBearing on walls said bearing and "unset" on unsaid.

## 6. What must not change
- No sizing, no span tables; every size typed with a status and a source ("an engineer sizes it: this is what was typed").
- Call 160's caution wording ("None of that is in this price unless a line of this job says so") and that nothing is priced as if solved.
- Unknown on an opened wall until said, and Assumed bearing still asked.
- Counted-not-sized joists with the line's words as the size and "blocking, hangers and doubled members are not in this count".
- Source line under every value; the Measure box that takes a laser reading; "Check" buttons.
- The count of members whose size is not Known on the sheet.
- A demolished wall in nothing's way once the work is done, but "in the way of demolition" kept as a separate list.
