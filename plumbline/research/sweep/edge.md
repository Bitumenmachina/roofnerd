### 1. Cloud2BIM (arXiv 2503.11498 v2, html full text read to char 100,000 of 105,334; PDF fetch unreadable)
Page: https://arxiv.org/html/2503.11498v2 (v2 18 Mar 2025). Code release named v1.02, "freely available" on GitHub.
Licence: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VaclavNezerka/Cloud2BIM/master/LICENSE (main branch path 404; master worked) reads "MIT License / Copyright (c) 2025 Václav Nežerka / Permission is hereby granted, free of charge...". First-pass claim "MIT" STANDS, now read from the LICENSE file. (Paper itself states no licence; its Table 1 lists dependency licences: Open3D MIT, NumPy BSD, IfcOpenShell LGPL.) Repo README also says MIT; 24 commits.
Method with every stated value (to check a builder's work against):
- Slabs: histogram of points along z, bin z_step = 0.05 m; a horizontal surface candidate = height range holding at least 50 % of the max point count of any single plane (max_n_points_array = 0.5). Assumes horizontal, uniform-thickness, step-free slabs. First slab and unpaired surfaces need manual thickness; an even count of surfaces means ceilings merged to form the plan. Plan outline: project to 2D, histogram, binary mask, dilation then erosion of 1 m each, OpenCV findContours (Suzuki-Abe), Douglas-Peucker with smoothing_factor 0.0005 m. Storey split keeps 0.1 m clearance (safety_margin).
- Walls: keep points at 90 to 100 % of floor height (z_section_boundaries [0.9,1.0]); "wall height is assumed to be the full height of the floor". 2D density histogram, binary mask threshold 0.01, square(5) kernel, contours, Douglas-Peucker epsilon 0.02 m/px (must not exceed the shortest segment to keep), merge collinear segments within angle_tolerance 3 degrees. Pair opposite parallel surfaces within a maximum distance with enough overlap; make a virtual surface if one side is missing. Longest segment sets axis direction; endpoints snap to intersections within half the max wall thickness. Paper gives NO values for min wall length, min/max wall thickness, exterior thickness.
- Rooms (zones): split wall axes at intersections, extend to neighbours within snapping_distance (no value given), offset by half wall width, extend to intersect, closed polygons = candidate zones. No voxels used.
- Openings: wall rotated into x-z plane; gaps in the x-histogram found with a threshold as a percentage of the 10th-highest bin (max10 = 10), repeated on z; heuristic rules on sill height, aspect ratio, min/max width (NO numeric values given); rectangular only. dilution_factor 10, min neighbour distance 0.01 m (synth3), 0.025 m (Kladno, Prague).
- Stated accuracy: synth3 distances point-to-wall "typically within millimeters", 13 of 13 doors found; Kladno station deviations up to 25 mm, over 50 mm at stairs/windows/furniture, rooms 24 of 25 (96 %); Prague hotel slab up to 25 mm, a few over 50 mm where points missing; ISPRS sets visual only. Speed (Core Ultra 165H, 32 GB): 6.9 M points 60 s; 2.55 M 30 s; 40.15 M points 30 min; claims ~7x Macher et al. throughput.
- Stated failures: no curved walls; rectangular openings only; one uniform height per storey (no steps, no varying ceilings); occlusion gives false openings (their Fig 23) fixed by hand; non-standard thickness and failed corner snapping merged two rooms; pairing/joining dominates runtime.
Verdict: first-pass "no accuracy numbers found" FALLS (numbers exist, above, mm to 25 mm on their sets); "histogram plus heuristics for openings" STANDS; "assumes regular grid" claim was about other work, not this. Note the key check for a builder: the thresholds above are the paper's, the unstated ones (thickness limits, snapping distance, opening size rules) are a builder's choice and must be recorded as Assumed parameters.
Not read: last 5,334 characters (likely references/appendix). Second classic paper: Macher et al. (MDPI Remote Sensing 2017) fetch REFUSED (HTTP 403); a non-neural classic paper NOT read in this pass.

### 1b. Two classic non-neural papers (PDFs fetched, text extracted locally with pdftotext and read)
A. Previtali, Barazzetti, Brumana, Scaioni, "Towards automatic indoor reconstruction of cluttered building rooms from point clouds", ISPRS Annals II-5, 2014 (Riva del Garda, 23-25 June 2014), doi 10.5194/isprsannals-II-5-281-2014. https://isprs-annals.copernicus.org/articles/II-5/281/2014/isprsannals-II-5-281-2014.pdf
 Method (its words/values): RANSAC planes, threshold epsilon 1 cm, normal threshold 20 degrees; bitmap cell size beta 1 cm (set to mean sampling resolution) and 5 cm for wall detection (its Table 1, same for both data sets). Rough floor plan = ceiling points projected to a binary occupancy raster ("ceiling is generally less influenced by clutter and occlusion"); a boundary counts as a wall only where a vertical plane segment falls on it. Missing walls: "pending" walls guessed orthogonal to detected ones; 2D cell complex; optimal binary empty/occupied labelling by min s-t cut (unary weights infinite for ceiling-occupied cells; edge weight = edge length, so it minimises length of guessed walls).
 Openings: "occlusions and clutter produce significant holes in the point cloud which have to be distinguished from real openings": per-wall-plane occupancy bitmap plus RAY-TRACING from each known scan position; each cell labelled empty / occupied / occluded; "a cell is considered as occluded if it is occluded in any scans"; openings read from labels. Needs scan positions known in advance.
 Stated accuracy: tested on a classroom 8 x 4.5 x 3 m (2 scans, ~28 M points each) and an office 12 x 8.5 x 3 m (3 scans, ~44 M points each), FARO terrestrial scanner (not a phone). Edge correct if within tolerance T of a manual model; "significant discontinuity at T = 4.0 mm"; commission and omission errors defined and plotted. Authors say manual operators can hardly beat 2-3 mm.
 Stated failures: "Legoland-world" assumption (rectangular walls; curved or triangular walls fail); pending walls assumed orthogonal, so floor plan may differ from reality.
 Relevance: this is the published basis for "no points is Unknown, not void": its three-state label (empty / occupied / occluded) is exactly Known / Unknown / absent. First-pass idea 8 STANDS and is now sourced to a read page. Code licence: none stated on the paper; not checked.
B. Ochmann, Vock, Klein, "Automatic reconstruction of fully volumetric 3D building models from point clouds", ISPRS J. Photogramm. Remote Sens. 2019 (arXiv 1907.00631, submitted 1 Jul 2019). https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.00631.pdf
 Method: RANSAC (Schnabel efficient RANSAC in CGAL) planes; ray-casting outlier removal (removes points below threshold 0.5; three ray-cast iterations); room segmentation on the unstructured cloud by clustering (MCL, inflation 2.0, patches 40 x 40 cm); walls as volumetric entities chosen by integer linear programming (Gurobi). Values: input subsampled to 2 cm min spacing; plane distance 1 cm; cluster epsilon 20 cm; normal threshold ~6 degrees (18 for one data set); min support 1000 points; miss probability 0.001; multi-label bitmap pixel 10 cm; occupancy bitmap 20 cm; surface cost weight alpha 0.04 (0.08 regularises more). Tools used: PCL, CGAL, MCL, Gurobi 8.0.1 (commercial solver) and NVIDIA OptiX GPU ray casting. Stated limit: "slanted walls, floors or ceilings are not taken into account"; openings and objects left as future work; regularising chains of almost-coplanar walls can fail. 
 Relevance: ILP + Gurobi is not buildable permissively as published (Gurobi is commercial; CGAL has GPL parts [INFER, not read]); the plane-fit and room-clustering ideas are. Slanted ceilings (a stated first-pass failure category) are confirmed as a stated limit here AND in Cloud2BIM.
