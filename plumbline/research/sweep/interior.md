# The interior: drywall, flooring and tile, paint, finish carpentry, kitchen and cabinets

Method: I read 33 pictures in `plumbline/demos/captured/` (listed under "What I saw"), ran the frozen command line (`sixteen`, `estimate`, `estimate by-item`, `takeoff`, `finishes`, `show`, `clashes`, `vif`, `items`, `cabinets`, `export supplier`) on a made-up whole house and on a 14 x 12 kitchen I built in my folder `.../sweep/interior/` (h.json, k.json). I did not run the window. "Not seen" means no picture shows it.

Prior notes I build on, not repeat: `research/trades-use-envelope-finishes.md` (patching by SF, split rooms, cabinet runs, countertop SF/LF, trim by LF, packs, 4x12, tile setting materials, wet-wall board). Where the program has moved since (cabinet runs now exist), I say what I found.

## 1. Who I am and why I open this

Five people at one table, same question: "what is on every wall, ceiling and floor of each room, how much, and what is in the way?"

- **Drywall contractor.** I come to get, room by room, wall SF by real ceiling height, ceiling SF, board by type and length (4x12, 5/8 type X at the garage wall, moisture-resistant at the bath, cement board behind tile), and above all what is new against what I must patch into. A renovation is won or lost on patch, feather and match. I also want to know what blocks the board: a duct chase, a plumbing wall that is not yet framed.
- **Flooring and tile setter.** Floor by room and by finish, with the transition where one finish meets another, tile on walls (tub surround, shower, backsplash), height to which it runs, the substrate (underlayment, backer, waterproofing membrane) and the step at the join with old floor.
- **Painter.** Wall SF, ceiling SF, trim LF, doors and windows EA, by room, by coat, with a coverage and a gallon count; and "what is already painted and what is raw".
- **Finish carpenter.** Base by the foot cut at openings, casing by the opening (3 sides or 4, LF), door and window schedule with swing, crown, stair parts, built-ins, shelving; and where the electrical, vents, and switches fall on the wall I am to trim.
- **Kitchen and cabinet designer** (lays out and orders his own cabinetry; the item library is his tool chest). I come to lay out runs on real walls with real windows, see clearances (range to wall, dishwasher beside sink, door swings, fridge depth), get the cabinet order by box code with fillers, end panels, toe-kick and crown, countertop SF and edge LF with cutouts, and what the wall hides (a pipe, a switch, a vent).

### Numbers from the command line (made-up whole house, h.json)

- `takeoff` gives per level wall faces gross/net by wall type, floors, rooms (inside walls); no ceilings, no per-room wall area line in the takeoff (the room has `wall_area`, e.g. Kitchen 416.89 SF, Bath 256.70 SF, but nothing prints it in takeoff or estimate).
- Drywall and paint are priced **by wall, not by room**: south exterior wall 329.99 SF net (the same figure as the siding), partitions "both sides" 307.7 SF at $4.80. A partition between a bath and a hall puts the bath-side board on the bath's estimate? No: it lands on the wall; the room's walls are therefore never summed. The divisions README itself says "a job priced both ways buys the board twice".
- The **room's ceiling** line is "Ceiling, gypsum board and paint" at $3.40/SF, one lump of board+paint over `floor_area`, with no ceiling height, no waste, no sheets (STATUS J-D7 open).
- **Base is priced on `perimeter`, not on the base that is drawn.** Kitchen: priced 50.25 LF, base run drawn 45.08 LF "none across a door". Living room: priced 110.125 LF vs 99.28 LF drawn. Over the ten rooms the job buys 550.5 LF; the drawn total is smaller by about 10%. Bath: priced 30.375, base run in the model 21.575 LF (the line the model works out) vs 27.88 LF in `finishes`. Three different base figures for one room (perimeter 30.375, wall run 27.875, base run 21.575) with the dollars on the largest. That is a real overbuy, and the 3D picture shows the shorter.
- Flooring: hardwood in kitchen and utility, tile in bath, flat priced SF = floor_area (no waste, no boxes, no transition at the door where hardwood meets tile, no underlayment, no thinset/grout). The bath has tile floor only; the "wet wall" is gypsum both sides, no backer, no tile, no tub surround, no waterproofing membrane.
- Doors: interior door at $640 + hardware $185 + "Casing and trim, 1 EA, $120" per door; windows get "Flashing and sealant", "Window treatment" but no interior casing/stool/apron line. Door height 6.67 ft (6'8"), width 3 ft; no swing, no hand, no jamb width for a 6 in wall.
- Kitchen in the sample: "Cabinets and countertops 1 EA $18,500" and "Kitchen appliances 1 EA $8,500". A lump. The placed-box path exists but the sample house does not use it, so the sample's kitchen cannot be asked "how many LF of base, how many wall boxes".
- Wall height is the level height (10 ft) in the first floor; second floor is 10 ft; a framed floor is 12 in thick, so the real ceiling is 10 ft less the floor and plate; unclear whether the model subtracts (not seen).

## 3. What is missing or wrong for me (one line each)

1. Looked for: base the carpenter buys. Got: base priced on room perimeter (kitchen 50.25 LF) while the model knows base run (45.08 LF, cut at doors, less cabinets). Why it matters: about 10% of the trim bought twice; the owner reads the drawn base and the sheet disagrees with itself.
2. Looked for: a room's walls and ceiling as the drywall bid (SF by room, by finish, tile vs paint). Got: board and paint by wall; the room's `wall_area` exists but is shown nowhere I could find; partition cost not split by the room on each side. Matters: I cannot send the painter "bath, 256.7 SF" and cannot price by room or phase it.
3. Looked for: wall tile (tub surround, shower, backsplash, wainscot) and cement board. Got: tile floor in bath only; "wet wall" is gypsum both sides. Matters: the most expensive finish in a bath is missing; a failed inspection/callback if plain board is priced behind tile.
4. Looked for: moisture-resistant or type X board. Got: one line "Gypsum board, hung and finished". Matters: garage and fire-wall board cost, and code.
5. Looked for: patching and the existing side of a wall. Got: Existing/Demolish/New work on a thing, but no patch SF and no feather or "match existing finish" quantity (already said in the prior notes; still open as of the pictures). Matters: patch is the biggest unknown on a renovation.
6. Looked for: the floor finish drawn per room and the transition between two. Got: a gray floor in the walk view; "Hardwood flooring", "Tile flooring" as priced lines only. Matters: the setter cannot see where the tile ends; no transition strip LF; no step at the old/new floor.
7. Looked for: a ceiling. Got: the walk view shows sky; the room has a ceiling only as a priced line. Matters: a room does not read as a room without a lid; painter and drywall cannot check heights; soffits and beams invisible.
8. Looked for: interior casing, stool and apron on windows, crown, chair rail. Got: door casing as 1 EA $120 each; nothing on windows; no LF. Matters: the finish carpenter prices casing by the foot and by profile; windows are about half the casing.
9. Looked for: switches, outlets, vents and recessed lights on the wall, with their heights. Got: Electrical is a separate switch; in the walk picture nothing visible on the wall; the open items show NEC paragraphs. Matters: the painter cuts around them, the tiler must hole-saw them, the cabinet run must clear them.
10. Looked for: cabinet door swing, appliance clearances, working triangle, landing space beside range/fridge. Got: boxes refused only for window/wall collision. Matters: the designer sells layouts that must pass; a 36 in refrigerator in front of a door is a change order.
11. Looked for: cabinets with toe kick, fillers by LF, end panels, crown, hardware, door style, and countertop edge LF, cutouts (sink, cooktop) and backsplash. Got: boxes EA and top SF; front edge LF appears in the message but not in the supplier export. Matters: half of a kitchen order is not boxes.
12. Looked for: cabinets that show as cabinets (doors, drawers, kick, top overhang). Got: a tan bar in 3D. Matters: the owner and the client cannot judge a layout; no "elevation" of a run (what a kitchen designer hands the client).
13. Looked for: the sample house's kitchen using the new cabinet runs. Got: "Cabinets and countertops 1 EA $18,500" and "Kitchen appliances 1 EA". The two methods do not meet: if the designer places a run, does the 1 EA lump still price? Matters: the same cabinets bought twice (same warning as board).
14. Looked for: finishes by room schedule (the architect's finish schedule: floor, base, wall, ceiling per room). Got: `finishes` lists walls, doors, windows, rooms' base only, not floors, walls, ceilings. Matters: this is the one sheet all five trades read.
15. Looked for: the packs the trades buy: sheets of 4x12, gallons, boxes of tile/flooring, linear pieces of base. Got: SF and LF. Matters: the order is wrong by waste and pack size (already noted; unchanged in sixteen).
16. Looked for: a switch that shows the inside faces, labelled in my words. Got: "Architecture", "Finishes" (exterior). Matters: I will not find my work.
17. Words: "Architecture", "no figure", paragraphs of NEC text in Open items for a kitchen, "Assumed ... and 62 more for the same reason" are not my words and hide which box.
18. Looked for: an interior door's swing, and a pocket or bifold. Got: one door shape, a panel door. Matters: clear floor, trim, and whether the vanity door can open.

## 4. What the best tools show today

- **Chief Architect** lays out cabinets with automatic fillers (made between cabinet and wall within 3 in), end panels on exposed ends, and cabinet schedules; community threads show the weak points: auto fillers do not show on the schedule, and cabinet "height" includes the countertop (https://chieftalk.chiefarchitect.com/topic/41074-is-there-a-way-to-turn-off-automatic-cabinet-fillers/ , https://chieftalk.chiefarchitect.com/topic/12582-end-panels-on-cabinets/ , https://chieftalk.chiefarchitect.com/topic/39636-help-custom-cabinet-schedule/). BuildOS gets the codes and the supplier list right but has none of the auto fillers or end panels; F3 filler exists only as an item.
- **PlanSwift** markets drywall takeoff as "every wall measured, every opening subtracted automatically" (https://planswift.com/). **STACK** reviews note ceiling and wall takeoff sequential, special assemblies need setup (https://www.stackct.com/?p=6043). The published practice is: wall LF x per-room height less openings, and ceilings tracked as their own item since lids hang and finish at different rates (https://www.zztakeoff.com/trades/drywall-takeoff-software , https://www.jlconline.com/business/estimating-job-costing/an-easier-way-to-estimate-material_o/). BuildOS has the room `wall_area` (call 144) which matches this method, but does not show it; and its ceiling is a lump.
- I did not find, on the pages searched, any of those tools linking a room finish schedule to takeoff items; I could not verify it either way. Not from memory: left as "not found".
- **Where BuildOS could be ahead, not level:** (a) the program already knows one world: the same wall carries siding outside, board inside, a pipe and a cabinet; no takeoff tool shows "what the wall hides" while pricing it. A drywaller shown "this board is in the way of 3 things to be set in the wall first" is ahead. (b) Existing/new/demolish on every element plus Assumed/Known means a patch quantity can be "Assumed until measured", which no estimator in the list does. (c) Cabinets that refuse a window and shrink the base: tie it to clearances and door swing and no kitchen package other than a dedicated one does both with a price on the same object. (d) Layouts compared side by side with dollars (the Layouts tab): a kitchen designer's "option A/B" with the cabinet price and the countertop SF. It is built, I did not see cabinets in it.

## 5. My ten, in order

1. **Base priced from the base that is drawn.** The Base line takes `base_run` (cut at doors, less cabinets), not `perimeter`. Screen: "Base and trim 45.08 LF" on the kitchen, the same figure as the room panel and the 3D base. Done when `estimate` quantity equals `finishes` quantity for every room and a test says so.
2. **Interior finishes by room as the one schedule.** A "Room finishes" list (and a panel under the room): Floor (hardwood, tile), Base, Walls (paint, tile to 4 ft, tile to ceiling), Ceiling, each with SF/LF from the room's `floor_area`, `wall_area`, `base_run`, and each Known/Assumed. A click on a line opens its source. Done when the bath shows "walls: tile to 7 ft, 150 SF; paint, 107 SF".
3. **Drawn interior: floors, ceilings and a switch for each.** Switches "Floors", "Walls (inside)", "Ceilings" in the Show list in trade words; the floor shows the finish colour/pattern by room (hardwood plank, tile grid); a ceiling lid with a height; in the walk view the room reads closed. Done when trades/6 shows a wood or tile floor and a ceiling.
4. **Wall tile and wet-area assembly.** A "Tub surround / shower" and "Backsplash" assembly on a wall face with a height and a run: tile SF, cement board SF, membrane, trim LF, with the wet wall's plain board replaced. Done when a bath with a tub shows board type behind the tile and a setter's list (thinset, grout, backer).
5. **Patch and match on existing.** A line "Patch and match" by SF on an Existing wall or room, entered in SF on the line, Assumed until measured, in drywall, paint and flooring (feather at the step). Done when a wall marked Existing with an opening cut shows "patch 12 SF" and VIF lists it with dollars.
6. **Casing, crown and trim by the foot, windows and doors.** Opening casing by LF from the opening's size, interior stool and apron at windows, crown by room perimeter; trim LF listed by profile in the carpenter's supplier export. Done when the carpenter lens lists "Casing 3 1/2 in: 164 LF".
7. **Kitchen: clearances and door swing.** Door and appliance swing arcs on the plan; a clearance finding "dishwasher door swing hits range" or "landing less than 15 in beside the range", with the dollars of the change. Done when a refrigerator against a wall is flagged and clears when moved, live.
8. **A kitchen that looks like a kitchen, and an order that orders.** Boxes drawn with doors, drawers, toe kick, countertop slab with overhang; auto filler/end panel on an exposed end; crown, light rail, counter edge LF and cutouts; elevation view of one run. Order sheet by box code with finish and hinge side. Done when a run of 6 boxes plus a filler shows 7 lines and an elevation picture.
9. **Packs and waste on the order.** Board by sheets (4x8, 4x12, type X, moisture-resistant), paint by gallon, tile and flooring by box, base by 16 ft stick; waste shown. Done when the supplier export prints "Gypsum board, 5/8 in: 63 sheets 4x12" with the waste and the SF behind it on click.
10. **One kitchen, not two.** When a run is placed, the lump "Cabinets and countertops, 1 EA" is shown as covered (or refused) so the same cabinets are not bought twice; same for board priced by wall and by room. Done when `estimate` says "cabinets: by the boxes placed (13 boxes); the lump is off" and a gate checks no double count.

## 6. What must not change

- The cabinet language: codes B, SB, DB, W (width then height), T, F, BLB, LS, "the number is the width in inches". The plain refusals ("B99 is not in this job's items", "a wall box does not hang across a window").
- The run starts so many feet along the wall, from the corner, and says "1.75 ft left to the corner".
- Boxes refused across windows; base subtracted behind cabinets (41 LF from 50).
- Base cut at doors in the walk view; door and window casing and reveals as dressed.
- Existing / To demolish / New work on every element, and "Assumed until measured".
- Plan reads like a drawing; room names with SF.
- The Layouts side-by-side with the room matched by name.
- Honest "no figure" and "Left out of the job cost" lines; the supplier export "quantities only, none of our prices".
- Framing view with studs and headers (the drywall person's own picture).
