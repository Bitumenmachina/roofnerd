# Where the starter items connect: the sources (call 211, contract 211M)

Searched with plain trade words only ("lavatory rough-in dimensions supply height drain height", "water closet rough-in dimensions
12 inch flange centerline supply height from floor", "bathtub rough-in drain location ..."). Pages read on 2026-11-25. Every place
below is Assumed in the library: "a typical rough-in; the product is not chosen". The product's own rough-in sheet decides.
The figures are in the item's own frame: across from the middle (a plus is to the right looking at the front), back from the back
(the side that goes to a wall), up from the foot; every starter item here stands on the floor.

## Water closet (the item is 20 in wide, 28 in deep, 30 in high)
- Waste, 12 in from the back, on the middle, at the floor, faces down.
  https://www.finehomebuilding.com/project-guides/plumbing/installing-a-toilet : most toilets are roughed in "at 12 in. from the
  finished wall" (to the centre of the flange). Same figure: https://horow.com/blogs/guide/bathroom-rough-in-dimensions-plumb-guide-for-bathroom-plumbing :
  "the center of the toilet flange is 12 inches from the finished back wall."
- Cold water, 6 in to the left of the middle, 6 in up, in the wall (back 0), faces back.
  Fine Homebuilding, same page: the supply sits about 6 inches left of the flange centerline and about 6 inches above the finished
  floor. (horow.com says 6 in left and 7 in up; Kohler's sheet for one product says 2 5/8 in up and 5 1/8 in left,
  https://www.manualslib.com/manual/2196746/Kohler-K-3379.html?page=3: so the product decides; 6 and 6 is the first source's.)
  "Left" is taken looking at the toilet's front (the person's left as they face the toilet from the room), so across is -6 in. The
  sources do not say which way they look: that reading is the program's, said here.
- Not written: a size for either (no source fetched for the flange's or the supply's size).

## Lavatory (20 in wide, 18 in deep, 34 in high)
- Waste, on the middle, 19 in up, in the wall (back 0), faces back: https://www.rosenberryrooms.com/bathroom-sink-drain-rough-in/ :
  "The standard bathroom sink drain rough-in height is 18-20 inches from the finished floor to the drain centerline" (19 is the
  middle of that); the same range, "Drain height: 18-20 inches above the finished floor, centered under the sink", at the horow.com page above.
- Hot water and cold water, 21 in up, 8 in apart (4 in each side of the middle), in the wall, face back:
  rosenberryrooms.com: "Position hot and cold supply lines 20-22 inches from the finished floor." and "Maintain 8 inches
  center-to-center spacing between hot and cold supply lines." (21 is the middle of 20 to 22; horow.com gives 22-24 in its
  summary and 2-3 in above the drain in its text: the sources disagree, which is why every place is Assumed.)
  Which side is hot (here the left, -4 in, the usual way in the US) is a convention the pages read do not state; it is the program's.
- Not written: sizes.
