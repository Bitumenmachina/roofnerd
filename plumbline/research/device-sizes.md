# Typical sizes for the appliances and small devices of the tool chest (call 233, contract 233M)

Every size is Assumed in the table and none names a maker. A size a published page gave says "a typical size; the product is not
chosen"; **the six no page gave say "an assumption: no published size was found"** (call 235): the depth of the receptacle, the
switch, the supply register and the return grille, and the height of the ceiling light and the recessed light. Searched 2026-11-26 with
plain trade words. Where a figure is from a published page the address and the line it says are quoted; where none was found it
is said so. The seven items that were boxes (range, refrigerator, dishwasher, washer, dryer, air handler, panelboard) were not
resized: their sizes are the chest's from before.

## What could not be sourced

A published page for the thickness of a wiring-device plate and the projection of its device face (NEMA WD 6 is paywalled); for the
height of a ceiling light; for the height of a recessed light's housing; for the projection of a register or a grille. These
sizes are Assumed like the rest and are named here as assumptions rather than figures from a page. A person who knows them better
changes the row: it is their table.

## Connections

None added. A published rough-in is the rule of call 211 and none was found for a shower base's drain or for a register's duct
opening that places it on the item; the register's duct opening is the nominal size above, centred on its face, and no place is invented.
