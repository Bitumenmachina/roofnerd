## Per item
1. DASHED LINES (fixed). Cause: the reader kept a dashed line whole as any other line and told the finder nothing of dashes, so a dashed pair 8 in apart was a wall like any (and a dashed ring a box that swallowed the plan). Fix: `Page.dashed` (their places among `lines`, lib.rs); the finder takes them out of the walls' lines altogether and looks at them only for what they may be: pairs by the rule for a wall's two lines become `Drawn.overhead` ("2 dashed lines 4" apart and 8' long, at X: something overhead or hidden (a beam, a soffit, cabinets above), or, on a plan of a renovation, a wall to be demolished; a wall's lines are solid, so it is not taken as a wall and not laid"), and every dashed line is counted ("N dashed lines (hidden or overhead, a property line, a wall to be demolished)"). RED: unit two_dashed_lines.../a_dashed_pair... (walls [16,16,16,18,36,36], wanted [16,16,16,36,36]: the pair laid as an 18 ft wall, doubt None)/a_dashed_property_line... (walls [], the ring a box); demo 8.1 (walls found: 7, 1 in doubt: wall 3 8 ft, 4 in). GREEN: both, and judge's f_beamdash 150.00 -> 135.00 LF.
   RENOVATION CONFLICT: on a renovation plan a dashed pair 4 1/2 in apart wall to wall is a wall to be demolished (my sheet t05). The call's rule wins: it is not found as a wall, not in doubt, not laid, and the sentence above says what it may be, with where. It never comes to the person as something to take (no --take): a person who wants the demolished wall in the job traces it. Whether the finder should offer dashed pairs on request (a flag, laid marked "demolish") is the lead's word, and Patrick's scope; the call as written needs no word.
4. PIER (fixed). Cause: a short piece between two openings was dropped twice: by the face (a piece under the height of a letter on the paper is no stretch) and by the pair (each piece had to be 2 ft). Fix: a piece on the line of a stretch that counts, within 16 ft of it (the widest opening), is a pier of it; the 2 ft is of the wall's whole line (first piece's start to last piece's end), also for the "short stretches" count. RED: unit a_door_beside_a_window... ("both found: [(true, 13.75, 18.75)]": the door gone), a_door_in_a_partition_two_feet...; demo 8.2 (door lost; the partition shortened 4 ft, its end "meets nothing"). GREEN: both; judge's pier75/100/150 1 -> 2 openings, d_b, d_c, big 434.67 -> 440.00 LF and 23 -> 25 openings (hand 440, 25).
2. A LINE IS THE FACE OF ONE WALL (first sentence fixed; second NOT, see conflicts). Cause: a face in two pairs over one stretch made both pairs "in doubt" (or, with one line, the doubtful pair pulled the neighbour's end 2 in short). Fix: pairs that share a line form a stack; the pair whose thickness most CLEAN walls of the sheet have claims the lines (ties: nobody claims, both in doubt as before); a pair that would use a claimed line again is in doubt, said once (the nearest pair for each such line): "another line lies beside it: one of its lines is already the face of a wall 6" thick, so it is not a second wall", and a wall in doubt for that reason no longer carries another wall's end to meet it. RED: unit a_line_that_is_a_walls_face... (walls [20,20,30], the north wall in doubt); demo 8.3b (walls found 5, 5 after: "laid ... 100.00 LF" failed). Judge's s01: 5 clean walls 104.83 LF with the west wall 19' 10" -> 6 clean 135.00 LF.
3. FRAMED BOX WORDS (fixed); APART FROM THE PLAN (NOT done, see conflicts). Boxes now say "(a title block, a key, a schedule or a detail, or a house or room drawn with one line for each wall, from which a thickness cannot be read: sizes)".
5. PICTURE AND CUT-OFF (fixed). Cause: nothing told the finder where a clip or the page's edge cut a line, nor how many pictures the page shows; a wall ending at the clip met nothing and was in doubt, and the closing sentence looked only at the clean walls. Fix: `Page.cuts` (where a clip or the page's edge cut a line off) and `Page.pictures` (lib.rs, the lines it already reads); the finder's `cut_ends` (an end that meets nothing within a thickness of a cut, for every wall, in doubt or not); the verb: "not read: N pictures on the page: nothing was read from them for walls...", "the walls found cannot be said to close: N ends run to the edge of what the page shows ... wall 1, its east end, cut off at X", the closing sentence is not said when there is a cut end, and "nothing else on the sheet was left out" is not said when there is a picture ("nothing else of the lines read was left out (nothing was read from the picture)"; a closed set of lines beside a picture is said to close "of the lines read"). RED: unit walls_run_to_the_edge...; demo 8.6. GREEN: judge's clip, halfvector, k12 (page edge), halfscan.
7. HEADS (fixed). Cause: the verb parsed any length. Fix (verb only; the scan's find untouched): a door's head must be above the floor; a window's sill at or above it and its head above it; the head-above-sill refusal is unchanged. RED: demo 8.7 (accepted); GREEN.

A sheet with no lineup says "the sheet has not been lined up with the job: its lower left corner is taken as 0, 0, so these places are measured from that corner of the page and not from the job's own origin (line it up first with plumbline lineup...)" and, when laying, "laid at the places said above, because ..."; a name that is no drawing of the job and not a file on hand says `"A-104" is no drawing of this job and no scan is on hand by that name; the job's drawings are: "A-103" (drawing-0003)`. A scan file that exists goes the scan's way exactly as before.

   a. Layers: a stack whose outermost two lines are a wall's thickness apart, with at least one line between, and whose outer thickness another wall or stack of the sheet also has, is ONE wall, faces the outer two, "drawn with N lines" on its line and said once in a sentence about the sheet. A stack on its own (nobody else has that thickness) is not taken as layers: it is as before, in doubt, said. (A wall with ONE line down its middle is still call 181's set of three evenly spaced lines: kept because the existing test three_lines_six_inches_apart_are_a_set_not_a_wall says so; the false sentence "no two lines 3 in to 14 in apart..." is replaced by one that says the lines are in sets, with where.) A plain rectangle of single lines is a closed box and is said as a box whose words now name a house drawn so.

## For the lead (decisions, blunt)
- ITEM 2, SECOND SENTENCE ("a pair whose thickness no other wall on the sheet has is in doubt"): NOT DONE. It breaks existing tests that must pass as written: check_house (the shared house: ring 6 in, ONE partition 4 1/2 in: unique) in the_house_with_a_partition_a_door_and_a_window_is_found_at_four_scales_and_turned, and walls_three_and_a_half_and_twelve_inches_thick_on_one_sheet (one 3 1/2 in partition among 12 in walls). Geometry cannot tell the judge's 6 in ledge (between an 8 in house and 4 1/2 in partitions) from the tests' 4 1/2 in partition between 6 in walls: both are one pair, touching walls at both ends. What I did instead (the claim by the thickness most clean walls have) handles the judge's counter/face case; the ledge f_bench and the shelves f_shelfbox/f_shelfopen are STILL laid as walls (unchanged). Options for the lead: (a) change those tests' expectations (a decision first); (b) a narrower rule such as "unique and thicker than the thinnest wall found" (catches the ledge 6 in and the 12 in shelf, spares thinner partitions; arbitrary and I did not build it).
- ITEM 3, "APART FROM THE PLAN": NOT DONE in the main patch. Read to the letter ("the plan is the largest group of walls that meet one another; walls apart from it are in doubt") it breaks the existing test two_houses_in_one_line_are_not_one_wall_with_a_door_twenty_feet_wide (a 20 by 12 and a 24 by 16 ring, each 4 walls, asserted all clean). OFFERED: call186-item3-offered.patch (drawn.rs, drawn_tests.rs only), with ONE measure, APART_SHARE = 0.5: a group is apart from the plan only if its walls total under half the plan's. It passes every existing test (the two houses are 64 and 80 LF) and its own test. Measured on the 67 judge sheets and my 10: secnoframe 8 walls/153.92 LF -> 6 walls/135.00 LF (section in doubt, said "apart from the plan"), t10 (my L plan and section) 136.25 -> 124.00 LF (hand 124), twoplans (the judge's 7-wall house and a 4-wall 12 by 10 one) the second house goes into doubt (said apart from the plan, not laid), nothing else changes. Apply it if the lead accepts "two plans of like size are both plans" and that a small second plan is asked about.
- Dashed pairs on a renovation plan (item 1): see above; the finder never offers them.
- The 3-line (or 2-line glass) window still gives a phantom "wall in doubt" per window, and several windows in one wall bridge into one (judge's findings 12, and my t07: 12 in masonry with glass lines 4 in apart): not in call 186; untouched. My change to the 2-ft rule lets glass pairs across several windows group up to 16 ft apart into one doubtful "wall" (it was already so for 2-3 windows 16 ft apart; it is in doubt and not laid).
- A wall with one line down its middle (judge's wallmid) is still lost, now said truthfully (sets). Call 181's rule; the existing test.

## Every measure chosen (named constants with reasons in drawn.rs, new in this call)
THICK_SAME 1/4 in (walls are one thickness within it: the claim by the thickness most clean walls have, and layers need another wall or stack of the outer thickness); piers reuse OPENING_MOST (16 ft: the widest opening) and the pair's 2 ft is of the wall's whole line; the overshoot of a cap and the distance of a jamb from a piece's end are the wall's own thickness (the call's words); a cut end is within a thickness + CAP_TOL of a place where a line was cut; a single-line outline is 3 or more long lines whose ends meet within CAP_TOL; SCALE_NEAR 1 per cent (the call's) with the lists of scales in world/plans.rs. Offered patch only: APART_SHARE 0.5.

## Where it will still fail on a real architect's sheet (blunt, new and kept)
- Layers: a stack with a thickness no other wall or stack has is not taken as layers (the only layered wall on a sheet, or a sheet of mixed layerings, is "in doubt, said", as before). A centre line down a wall is a set (call 181); a wall drawn with a hatch of parallel lines at 1/4 in spacing is lines in sets.
- Dashed: a dashed linetype exploded into separate short strokes (CAD does this) is not a dash_array: it is lettering-sized "too short" lines, never walls and never said as dashed pairs.
- Cuts and pictures: the reader hands places where a clip or the page's edge cut a line, and a count of pictures; not where a picture is. A page with a picture over half of it says so once.
- Another plan or a section at another scale on a sheet is still laid when it is large or meets the house (item 3 not in the patch).
- Scale caution needs the sheet to be a PDF; a picture has no paper size. A drawing set to a scale from a long dimension typed in feet and inches wrongly rounded is cautioned only if it is not within 1 per cent of a real scale (a 0.8 per cent error passes).
- Not measured on a million-line sheet; the dashed split adds one more faces pass over the dashed lines only.
