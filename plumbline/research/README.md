# Research and judgments kept from the build

Brought into the repository on 2026-11-13 so that it travels with a copy of it. Until
then these sat in a scratch folder outside it. Nothing here is real job data: the
surveys are of public programs and of the engine's own source, and the judges worked
on made-up jobs.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `window-survey.md` | How Google Earth, Cesium and building programs move, scroll and layer glass, with the numbers taken (DECISIONS.md call 63 summarises it) |
| `engine-survey.md` | What the engine (Bevy 0.20.1) gives for glass, light, sky, shadows and picking, by file and line of its source |
| `judgment-19-brief.md`, `judgment-19-notes.md` | The nineteenth judgment (the rebuilt window): what the judge was asked and its 51 notes as written. STATUS.md has the report and where each note stands |
| `judgment-20-brief.md`, `judgment-20-notes.md` | The twentieth judgment (foundation, framing, waterproofing, interior woodwork, the typed-length fix). **Stopped part way** on 2026-11-13 to save the session's limits: nine findings written, and a test job laid out with what the judge expected to find and had not yet looked at. None of it is fixed. STATUS.md lists them |

Paths in these files that begin `/tmp/claude-0/` are the scratch folder of the session
that wrote them and do not exist on another machine. `../divisions/README.md` is the
index by division.
