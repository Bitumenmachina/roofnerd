anywhere**. The mark rule part A was to build is not on screen.

Figures that do not carry what they should:
1. **$468,050.22** (top right, x 1380-1478, y 23-37, all 1600/1366 rest and work):
   no mark, no name. It rests on 85 things still to verify (Assumed) and on made-up
   prices; it reads as Known. The old window wrote "PRICE $468,050.22" in amber with
   "Assumed". This is the program's first rule broken on its biggest number. Also no
   name: a person cannot tell it is the price of the job (the word "Price" is gone)
   rather than, say, the cost.

   new price card says nothing about what the total leaves out. If lines are still
   unpriced in this job (the same sample house), the total now leaves something out
   silently (fixed rule: "A total that leaves something out says what it left out").
   I cannot tell from the picture whether they still are; the builder must show it.
   "$27,058.62 riding" (dollars on the verify list) is gone too.

   price", "Extension $1,150.00", "Unit cost $65.00/EA": a made-up price is not Known,
   and an extension of it is not Known: each should carry the Assumed mark; none does.
   "Opening 25.00 SF" comes from a traced window size: in the old window the same kind
   of quantity (a wall's face) carried "Assumed"; here it has no mark.
4. "85 to verify" is written in amber lettering: a coloured word, not a mark before an
   ink figure, against the rule part A was to build.
5. Level heights "10' 0"", "0' 0"", "-4' 0"" and the plan's "40' 0"", "28' 0"": no mark;

   count (things? lines?). Minor.
7. Readout "E 22' 0 3/16" N 6' 1 1/16" H 22' 6 1/2"": what point this is (the pointer)
   is not said.

Words that are not a builder's:
