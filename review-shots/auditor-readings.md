# Auditor readings of the gate C pictures (opus, one agent per picture, handed the PNG and one line naming the screen, no STATUS.md)

## S1

1. WHAT IS ON IT: An empty three-pane desktop-app shell branded "roofnerd", tagline "Trace the roof on one screen, watch the money on the other." Top bar: File menu, "no file open", a "Roof area —" readout, a "Showing: Full estimate" chip, help (?) and dark-mode icons. Below it a dimmed hint strip, "Tool settings appear here once a tool is active". Center canvas is blank white with a narrow empty left gutter (tool rail, no icons), a Plan / Model / Section view toggle top-right captioned "one geometry · three views", and two buttons in the middle: primary "Open a job" and secondary "Open the demo job". Right panel: tabs Estimate (active) / Condition / Recap (both greyed), a header row reading "nothing traced yet — 0 lines", column heads ITEM / QTY / COST, the placeholder "Cost shows up here as soon as there is something traced. Nothing to price yet.", and a footer "Selling price — nothing priced yet". Bottom status bar: Cursor —, Slope —, "Insulation here —", Flow — (Flow is highlighted blue), and a trailing bare "—".

2. WHAT THE ESTIMATOR WOULD DO NEXT: Load the roof plan they were sent — a PDF or TIFF of the architectural roof plan — then set drawing scale and start tracing the perimeter. The screen offers the load step ("Open a job", plus File), but ambiguously: "a job" reads like a saved roofnerd project file, not "import my plan PDF", so a first-timer with only a PDF in hand cannot tell whether that button is for them. Nothing on the screen mentions a plan, drawing, PDF, or scale. The safer first move — "Open the demo job" — is offered and is genuinely the strongest thing here, since it lets them see the trace-to-money loop before committing their own file.

3. WHAT IS WRONG, UNCLEAR OR MISSING:
- "Open a job" vs "Open the demo job" does not distinguish "import a new plan" from "reopen saved work". There is no third path for the actual first-run case: start a new job from a plan PDF. No drag-and-drop target or "drop a plan PDF here" affordance on the big empty canvas, which is the obvious place for it.
- No mention of scale anywhere, and scale-setting is the make-or-break first step in plan takeoff. A screen that never says the word invites a first trace at the wrong scale.
- Three separate "nothing yet" messages stacked in the right panel (nothing traced yet / Nothing to price yet / nothing priced yet) plus a fourth in the hint strip. Redundant, and it makes the empty state feel broken rather than ready.
- The units question is unanswered: "Roof area —" with no unit, and commercial estimators work in squares vs sq ft. Bottom bar shows "Insulation here —", a label that reads like leftover placeholder text, and a trailing bare "—" with no label at all — looks unfinished.
- "Flow" is styled blue (active/selected) while everything else is grey, implying a mode is already engaged before any click, with nothing explaining what Flow is. Unexplained jargon on a screen with zero state.
- Condition and Recap tabs are greyed with no tooltip or hint as to what unlocks them, and no indication of what they contain — an estimator cannot judge whether the tool covers their scope.
- The left tool rail is completely empty, so the screen gives no preview of what tracing tools exist (perimeter, penetration, drain, parapet). Combined with the blank canvas, the app looks capability-less until a file is open.
- Plan / Model / Section is presented before there is anything to view, and "one geometry · three views" is a product-positioning claim, not an instruction — it occupies prime real estate that could carry the actual first step.
- No recent-files list, no sample of what a finished estimate looks like, no version/build marker, and the "?" icon is the only route to help with no label or tooltip visible.
- Title bar says "no file open" in the same low-contrast grey as disabled controls, so the single most important piece of state — you have nothing loaded — is the least visible text on the screen.

**(3) What is wrong, unclear or missing.** The class arithmetic all ties (every sub-line sums to its class; every $/SQ is the class over 96.00), but the totals do not foot cleanly: the six classes sum to $194,458.37 and the footer's own three groups ($120,860.58 + $39,062.64 + $34,535.15) also sum to $194,458.37, while Job cost prints $194,458.36 — yet Contract amount $223,627.12 equals $194,458.37 + $29,168.75, so the display is rounding the cost down while the waterfall carries .37; a penny, but it means the printed Job cost is not the number the rest of the column used, and an estimator who re-adds the page will not reproduce the total. Escalation is computed on the pre-tax material base while tax is computed on the same base, so neither compounds — defensible, but undisclosed, as is the escalation's date basis. The 96.00 SQ divisor is the plan area with no waste factor, so every $/SQ is a plan-area figure masquerading as a coverage figure. Zero hours are shown against Material, Sub, Equipment and Other, which reads as data rather than as not-applicable.
